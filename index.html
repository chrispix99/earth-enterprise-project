In memory of

The Earth Enterprise Project

The life’s work of Joan Pick (1940–2017) — published here in full, every surviving page.

Joan

Joan Pick (1940–2017) lived in East Croydon for the last fifty years of her life, where local children knew her as “the running lady” — breathlessly brushing past them on foot. She was a tenacious and dedicated campaigner for sustainable economic policies, who gave outstanding service to the community she lived in.

She mothballed her car in 1972. In the remaining forty-five years of her life she used motorised transport twice, and only when extreme circumstances required it. She tried to avoid any use of gas, electricity or oil — living, decades early, the very principles her writing argued for.

In 2024 ECCO planted a lime tree in her memory at the Bisenden bed, at the junction of Bisenden Road and Addiscombe Road, and a wood-turned plaque celebrating her life and work was installed at a community celebration on 6 March 2024. (ECCO’s celebration of Joan’s legacy.)

She signed her work “Miss J M Pick”. In her final year she re-typed her deteriorating 1970s letters and papers to preserve them — the archive below holds those retypes alongside the foxed originals. Every page was photographed on 5 January 2017 and is published here by her nephew, Chris Pick.

The manuscript

What survives of the Earth Enterprise papers falls into three parts: the typewritten contents of the original manuscript, the printed INTERPLAN pilot-study booklet, and Joan’s correspondence. Click any page to read it full-size.

The typewritten contents 2 pages

The original typewritten contents pages: Part One — An Interim Report on a Structure for the Energy Economy (sections 1–7 and appendix), followed by Parts Two to Four — The Management Reaction; The Attitude of the Consumer; A Discourse on the Theory of Options and Constraints — dated 7 November 1975. The body of the typewritten manuscript itself does not survive in this archive.

An Interim Report on a Structure for the Energy Economy 75 pages

This was the pilot study itself: Joan worked at Interplan, and the Earth Enterprise Project was the pilot study she produced there. Six pages of front matter (June 1977 Summary of Progress to the Board, 10 June 1977 note to the Trustees, cover, April 1973 title page, two contents pages), then the complete text, pages 1–69. © 1973–77 Interplan Research Limited and Miss J M Pick. (Chapter 7, the appendix on number theory, was not photographed.)

Correspondence & loose pages 18 pages

Letters and papers in chronological order: a note to the reader from Interplan House; 1974–75 correspondence with Prof. Eric Laithwaite, Sir Alan Cottrell, the BBC (declining a TV feature), the Industrial Economist and McKinsey — originals and Joan’s 2016 retypes; a 1 July 2016 letter to Dr Henry Kissinger headed “The Earth Enterprise Project”; her October 2016 extrapolation of the business model and final draft to the Board; and retyped contents pages.

Published September 2026 by Chris Pick, Joan’s nephew. Photographs of every surviving page taken 5 January 2017.
